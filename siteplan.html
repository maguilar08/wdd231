Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help people find places to visit during weekends or holidays. The site will provide suggestions for tourist destinations, fun activities, and recommended restaurants. It will also show the best time to visit certain places and useful information to plan a great day out with family or friends.

Audience

This site is designed for families, couples, and groups of friends who are looking for ideas on where to go during their free time. It is simple, responsive, and easy to use on both mobile phones and computers.

Scenarios

  • Where can I go this weekend with my family that is fun and affordable?
  • What are the best restaurants near popular tourist places?
  • When is the best time to visit certain destinations during the year?
